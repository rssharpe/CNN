Current capabilities
--------------------
- Up to three SCC COM sources, playback, counter simulation and view-only
  TCP cable-counter clients.
- Separate optional ATO tension panel with its own RS-485 COM connection
  and Modbus settings. It can be hidden without disconnecting the source.
- Tension simulation, display zero/reset, editable label, peak tension,
  warning and critical thresholds with selectable colors, and optional
  critical sound.
- A selected cable source/address can be paired with tension for a live
  tension-versus-cable plot and tension CSV logging.
- Tension remains local to the connected CCN window; it is not currently
  sent to TCP clients.
